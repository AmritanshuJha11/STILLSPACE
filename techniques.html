Meditation techniques

Breath awareness

Use the feeling of each breath as a place to return.

  1. Sit comfortably and let your shoulders settle.
  2. Notice the air moving in and out, at your own pace.
  3. When your mind wanders, gently return to the next breath.

Body scan

Move your attention through the body, one small region at a time.

  1. Sit or lie in a position you can hold comfortably.
  2. Start at your feet. Notice contact, warmth, or stillness.
  3. Move slowly up through your legs, torso, hands, and face.
Try a guided session

Mindful listening

Let the sounds around you become the focus of your practice.

  1. Find a comfortable spot and let your gaze soften.
  2. Notice a nearby sound, then a sound farther away.
  3. Listen as each sound changes and fades.
Try a guided session

Walking meditation

Use the rhythm of your steps to anchor your attention.

  1. Choose a clear, familiar path and keep your eyes open.
  2. Notice one foot lifting, moving, and meeting the ground.
  3. Continue at an easy pace, returning your attention to each step.
